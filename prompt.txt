Build a program that writes one 1024x1024 image summing up world news of
Wednesday 25 November 2026: a dense newspaper front page with a giant serif
date masthead, a UTC timeline, a numbers strip, 3 lead story cards and 10
smaller cards (each with a small flat illustration drawn in code that
carries a fact), a world map with numbered pins, 23 more stories as
one-liners, and a DATE CHECK box naming stories left out because they broke
on another day. 36 verified stories from 25 November only, 7 regions,
confidence badge with source on every card. Everything drawn in code with
Pillow; the runner (main.py, standard library only) verifies the bundled
entry.png by SHA-256 and copies it to /output/entry.png. Deterministic:
running it twice produces byte-identical output.
